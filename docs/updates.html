Changelog

Recent feature work

Hand-written log of larger changes that span multiple commits.

May 2025

Arena 2 — classroom NPC system

Six teacher NPCs added to the Arena 2 classroom building: Frans (Mevr. Schaapherder), Wiskunde (Dr. Grin), Nederlands (Mevr. Huiskamp), Natuurkunde (Dr. Aalberts), Biologie (Mevr. Klever), and Engels (Mr. Daniel). Each teacher has a unique texture generated from the base character skin, a fixed classroom position derived from the schematic, and six lines of subject-specific educational dialogue. Teachers despawn during active waves and respawn with a live-check loop every 3 seconds.

May 2025

Boss — Julian AI rewrite (phase state machine)

Julian's hot chocolate attack was rewritten from a simple boolean flag (_julian_pour) to a proper phase state machine (_julian_phase = "normal" / "pour"). The new version adds a particle shower cone and correctly resets between pours. The old dead-code path was removed from boss/init.lua.

May 2025

BGM — dynamic loop system for wave music

Wave music now plays through a structured sequence: intro (20 s) → begin2 (30 s) → a shuffled loop of seven middle/bridge tracks. Per-player handles mean each connected player gets their own volume control. Players inside Arena 2 or riding Teinetarnagh are exempted from Arena 1 audio entirely.

April 2025

Trailer mode — camera wall avoidance

The cinematic camera now probes for solid nodes ahead and to the sides using multi-distance ray checks. Velocity is adjusted in real time to avoid clipping into arena walls during recording. The /trailer wave N command was added to start any specific wave without waiting for the time trigger.

April 2025

Arena 2 schematic — room position correction

Room positions were corrected by parsing the barlaeus_arena.mts schematic directly (MTS v4 format, zlib-compressed node data). Left rooms at world x = −320, right rooms at world x = −289. Three z-depths: −418, −400, −387.
