Lore & Technical Reference

The Dragons.

Three Dragons appear in Memento Mori. Each has a name, a role, and a distinct behaviour system. They share the same model (draconis_fire_dragon.b3d) but differ in texture, size, AI phase logic, and purpose. This page documents all three.

Dragon Profiles

Full documentation

Behaviour, stats, visual effects, and sound references for each Dragon.

Kearach

Hugo's summoned Dragon — Level 2 encounter

Blue Dragon Combat boss:summoned_dragon

Stats

  • HP: 50
  • Damage: 2 per hit
  • Visual size: 6 × 6 × 6
  • Collisionbox: −0.5 to 0.5 horizontal, 0 to 2.5 vertical
  • Physical: yes (gravity applies)
  • Glow: 8

Summoning conditions

  • Hugo reaches 25% HP and is not already in summon or linked phase
  • No Victory Dragon currently active (boss._victory_dragon_obj must be nil)
  • 3-second channeling phase before the Dragon appears
  • Hugo becomes invulnerable (linked phase) while Kearach lives
  • When Kearach dies, Hugo dies immediately too

Spawn visual effects

  • Vertical dark energy column: 80 particles, 2 seconds, rising velocity
  • Ground shockwave ring: 40 particles, 0.5 s
  • Aura particle colour: #386dff

Ongoing smoke trail

  • Emitted every 0.15 s from behind the Dragon
  • Particle colour: #6600AA:180
  • Secondary: #1a0033:200

Sounds

  • dragon_roar1 / dragon_roar2 — on hit (40% chance)
  • dragon_roar2 — on death (gain 7.0)
  • draconis_fire_breath.ogg — fire breath attack
  • hugo_karate1/2/3/4 — Hugo's power cry when triggering the attack phase

Drop

  • registered:sword_fire — fire sword drops on death
  • Note: the fire sword is a blacklisted weapon — it cannot be used in boss fights

Nametag note

The Dragon spawns with the initial nametag Kearach. The nametag changes to Kearach the first time the Dragon is damaged. Both names refer to the same entity.

Caeltaroch

Companion Dragon — follows and protects its owner

Slate Dragon Companion boss:companion_dragon

Properties

  • Texture: slate_dragon.png + slate_eyes.png + draconis_baked_in_shading.png
  • Visual size: 6 × 6 × 6
  • Physical: no (manual velocity)
  • Glow: 10
  • Nametag: Caeltaroch (#1869db)
  • static_save: false

Behaviour

  • Follows a designated owner (player name string stored in _owner)
  • Attacks enemies near the owner; has its own attack cooldown and roar timer
  • Tracks animation state in _current_anim to avoid restarting mid-play
  • Roar timer initialises at 5 s

Teinetarnagh

The Victory Dragon — spawns after all 7 waves are beaten

Black Dragon Victory boss:victory_dragon

Properties

  • Texture: black_dragon.png + draconis_baked_in_shading.png
  • Visual size: 8 × 8 × 8 (larger than other Dragons)
  • Physical: no (manual velocity, no gravity)
  • Collisionbox: −0.8 to 0.8 horizontal, 0 to 3.0 vertical
  • Glow: 8
  • Nametag: Teinetarnagh (#FFD700)
  • Armour: immortal (cannot be damaged)

Phase state machine

intro waiting takeoff flying landing landed

Starts in intro (2.5 s), transitions to waiting. Right-click to mount → moves to flying, climbs 22 nodes above current position, then descends to Arena 2 entry point and transitions to landing → landed.

Mounting

  • Right-click in waiting or landed phase to mount
  • Right-click again to dismount; also auto-dismounts on arrival
  • Player model is hidden while riding (collisionbox zeroed, visual_size zeroed)
  • Camera locked to Dragon at eye offset Y+920, Z−2240 (scale 8 formula)
  • Only one rider at a time: "Teinetarnagh heeft al een ruiter."
  • Cannot mount during flight to Arena 2: "Teinetarnagh is nog niet klaar om te rijden."

Destination

  • ARENA2_ENTRY: x=−303, y=179, z=−439
  • Dragon carries rider autonomously — no manual controls
  • Arrival announced in chat as [Teinetarnagh] message
  • On departure: "De strijd is gestreden. Beklim mijn rug." etc. (4 random lines)

Sounds

  • dragon_roar1 — on spawn
  • dragon_roar2 — on player mount
  • dragon_roar3 — on landing / arrival

API

  • boss._victory_dragon_obj — singleton objectref, nil when not active. Hugo's summon checks this before spawning Kearach.
  • boss.spawn_victory_dragon(pos) — spawns Teinetarnagh near pos, no-op if already active.
  • /spawn_victory_dragon — chat command (server priv), spawns at caller's position.

— Teinetarnagh, on victory

Model & animation reference

All three Dragons use the same skeleton and animation frame ranges.

Model

  • File: mods/boss/models/draconis_fire_dragon.b3d
  • Bone used for rider attach: Torso.2
  • backface_culling: disabled on combat Dragons

Animation frames

  • Walk: frames 211–249 @ 30 fps
  • Hover: frames 321–359 @ 30 fps
  • Other ranges tracked per entity in _current_anim to prevent mid-play restarts

Sound files

  • dragon_roar1.ogg
  • dragon_roar2.ogg
  • dragon_roar3.ogg
  • draconis_fire_breath.ogg

    • All located in mods/boss/sounds/.

Textures

  • blue_dragon.png — Kearach
  • slate_dragon.png + slate_eyes.png — Caeltaroch
  • black_dragon.png — Teinetarnagh
  • draconis_baked_in_shading.png — shared shading overlay on Teinetarnagh and Kearach
